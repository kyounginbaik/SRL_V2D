Smoke contract:
- 2,048 worlds, 524,288 main transitions, one upper round at 501,760.
  Same batch 1,024 and replay settings as full training, so GPU shape is tested.
- Upper: 56D local full-hand correction, one actor proposal per selected window,
  up to four windows chosen from baseline object failure/onset/history.
- Upper critic learns .01-scaled discounted wrench-support cost. Proposal
  gradients differentiate three temporary lower actor Adam updates starting
  from its actual incoming moments/count and learning-rate schedule. Production
  multipliers/projection are preserved; sqrt(v_hat+1e-12) regularizes zero-moment
  derivatives. Lower critics, temperature, statistics and experience stay frozen:
  this remains a finite actor-response approximation, not full SAC differentiation.
- Actual validation trains the full lower FlashSAC on fresh experience for
  both candidate and unchanged references, starting from the same learner.
- Adaptation uses three checkpoints chosen from the scoring interval: at least
  64 vector steps, then two 32-step increments, with 16 steps of desired tail.
  Strict cap: 448 vector steps (917,504 transitions per branch at 2,048 worlds).
  Early intervals can use 64 / 96 / 128 vector steps. Later windows receive more
  time within the cap. Real coverage and actor/critic learning remain required.
  All selected proposals are physically tested; no critic-ranked shortlist.
- Before proposals, the exact incoming controller's fresh unassisted baseline
  calibrates the critic for 32 updates. Proposal validation uses only that
  round's baseline generation. Old same-version baselines are excluded.
- At EVERY candidate/control checkpoint, the actual unassisted rollout trains
  the upper critic, regardless of later acceptance. Complete discounted returns
  default to MC weight1. For other weights, Huber applies to a blended TARGET;
  separate clipped TD/MC losses must not cancel. TD labels, MC targets and upper
  regularization all use scale=.01. Report explicit normalized/raw values.
  Recorded observations/actions stay paired with that reference and lower actor.
  Branch data never enter current-reference actor replay or get relabeled as
  accepted data. Trial datasets are discarded after fitting, not stored in a
  persistent cross-policy replay; upper models/optimizer states retain updates.
- The upper objective is measured full-horizon support cost. Early tracking
  resets have stale reference-frame terminal observations (lifecycle did not
  advance): mark critic_valid=false and omit them from upper TD fitting, but
  retain them for inner actor replay. Full-horizon baseline/trial MC supplies
  actual continued-failure evidence. Upper done marks only reference-horizon
  completion; lower FlashSAC termination behavior remains unchanged.
- Evaluate all three adaptation checkpoints and keep the best eligible pair
  per window. Require learning/coverage, all per-object and archived-prefix
  guards, and gain over both incoming and same-budget no-edit greater than
  max(.001, .005*abs(min(incoming_cost, control_cost))). Plateau/stability is
  diagnostic only; its success resolution remains max(.02,1/N).
- Rank eligible pairs by measured cost; confirm at most two until one passes.
  Freeze candidate, matched no-edit and incoming policies and repeat all three
  VOC=0 full-horizon rollouts from the same initial state. No further training
  or reset randomization. Worse original/repeat candidate must beat the better
  original/repeat score of BOTH comparisons, with the same margin and guards.
  At most six extra single-world rollouts; no added vector-training budget.
  Only the published proposal is marked accepted.
- Publish the accepted reference and complete adapted lower learning state
  together. A rejected proposal preserves the incoming learner and replay.
- Fixed initial physical state, immutable object targets, pot 0.2kg, lid 0.1kg,
  existing controller/rewards, VOC starting at 1 with performance gates.
- Keep videos, 45% original-hand overlays and six estimated Track 3 metrics.
